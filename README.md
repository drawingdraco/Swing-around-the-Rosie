Swing arround the ROssie was made for the october jame gam game jam hosted by hack club
Swing around the Rosie is a Foddian bullet hell where you play as a little guy swinging a scythe around to kill invasive plants. But you're so small the momentum of your swing also sends you flying.

 movement - WASD a/o arrow keys

swing scythe - click

roll - space

NOTE - you are only invincible during the swing.
